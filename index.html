About me:
curiosity, with a mathematical core.

A little about who I am, what I’m learning, and the questions that keep me curious.

Hi, I’m Sidharth — a student in the IIT Madras BS programme in Mathematics. I’m interested in how mathematical ideas can help us understand and build things, from algorithms and machine learning models to quantitative approaches to financial markets.

I like the mix of abstract thinking and hands-on problem-solving: understanding why a theorem works, finding a cleaner algorithm, or testing an idea with code. Right now, I’m building my foundations, learning consistently, and looking for opportunities to turn what I study into thoughtful projects.

Outside any single subject, I value curiosity, clear reasoning, and learning by doing. This website is my evolving notebook — a place to share what I’m exploring and the work I create along the way.

A small experiment in the spirit of this site: stretch the orbit and watch the planet speed up as it nears its star.

Orange: the area swept by the planet in its most recent moments. Cyan: the area swept in the same span of time half an orbit away. The shapes differ, but the areas are equal — Kepler’s second law.
